# public/assets — what is here, and what is deliberately missing

This directory is a straight copy of the legacy site's `assets/` tree. The
paths are absolute and **must not change**: `/assets/fonts/…`,
`/assets/brand/…`, `/assets/site/…` are hard-coded in `globals.css`, in the
`<link rel="preload">` tags in `app/layout.tsx`, and in ~2,900 `src`/`srcset`
attributes across the 178 legacy HTML pages that later agents port from.

## Copied

| Path | Size | Used by |
|---|---|---|
| `fonts/` | 68 KB | the two `@font-face` blocks in `globals.css`; preloaded on every page |
| `brand/` | 52 KB | logo (`topcat-horizontal.svg`, `topcat-vertical.svg`), `favicon.svg`, `topcat-icon.svg` |
| `site/` | 6.0 MB | hero stills, page heads, process/service/team photography, `plate-stone-700.webp`, `og-cover.jpg`, `manifest.json` |
| `projects/` | 6.7 MB | the `/projects/` case-study galleries |
| `team/` | 400 KB | `/about/` portraits |
| `stone-floor.webp` | 92 KB | `--marbleBG`, i.e. `body::before` on every single page |
| `kitchen-day.jpg` | 392 KB | 13 references across the legacy pages |

Font files are byte-identical to source: `cinzel-latin-var.woff2` 25,904 B,
`montserrat-latin-var.woff2` 37,956 B.

## NOT copied yet — served from the legacy paths for now

Two directories are excluded on purpose, because they are large and neither is
needed until the components that consume them exist:

| Path | Size | Consumer |
|---|---|---|
| `assets/video/` | **38 MB**, 9 references | the home-page cine film (`#hero`, `html.cine-on`) |

`assets/slabs/` **is now copied** — 265 files, 39 MB. The 134 `/stones/*` pages
landed and they are its consumer, so it was wired following the checklist
below: copied straight across, nothing renamed, nothing re-encoded. All 264
distinct `/assets/slabs/…` URLs in `src/data/stones.json` resolve to a file on
disk; the 265th is `.gitkeep`-style spare in the source tree. The home-page
stone wheel is the other consumer and needs no further copying.

Until it is wired, anything referencing `/assets/video/…` resolves against the
**legacy deployment's** own copy, which sits at the same absolute URL on the
SiteGround host next to `send.php`. Nothing breaks in production; what breaks
is a purely local `next dev` preview of the cine film.

### When you wire them up

1. Copy `../../assets/video` into this directory. Do not rename anything and
   do not re-encode — the film's frame timing (`FPS=60`, `DUR=44.25`) is
   measured against the exact encode that ships today. (`assets/slabs` was
   copied the same way: its filenames are keys in `src/data/stones.json`.)
2. The film work also carries the two measured perf fixes documented at the
   top of `src/app/globals.css` §6: memoise the `grade()`/`bandGrade()`
   canvas readback against the presented video frame, and replace the
   per-frame `video.currentTime` scrub with a forward-playing
   `playbackRate` chase.
3. Nothing else changes. `next.config.ts` already sets
   `images: { unoptimized: true }`, so these are copied to `out/` untouched.
