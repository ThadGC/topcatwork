What we did

A tired galley kitchen reimagined around a single bookmatched island — the veining runs unbroken from the waterfall edge, across the top, and down the other side.

We began with a full templating session, capturing every measurement to a fraction of a millimetre once the units were levelled. Our fabrication partners cut and polished the slab to that template, matching the veining across each join so the seams all but disappear.

The finished worktops were installed in a single day — squared, sealed and ready to use — with a full-height splashback tying the run together and a honed finish chosen to keep glare down under the pendant lights.
