London kitchens bring problems the Home Counties do not. Access is the big one: third floor flats without a lift, communal stairwells with tight turns, and streets where the van cannot sit outside for long. Those are planning problems rather than obstacles, and we would rather talk about them at survey than discover them on fitting day. Tell us the access when you enquire and we will plan the piece sizes and joint positions around it.

We work across Greater London regularly. For central postcodes we will talk through access, parking and permits before we quote, because those affect how the job runs. Three to five working days from template to fitting, access permitting.

How it works

Step 1

Free home visit

We come to you with samples, look at the actual room and talk through the stone, the edges and the layout. No showroom trip, and no obligation.

Step 2

One itemised price

Templating, stone, fabrication, all cut-outs, fitting and VAT, in one figure agreed before anything is cut. What you approve is what you pay.

Step 3

Template

Once your units are level we template the room by hand, to a fraction of a millimetre, and agree joint positions and veining direction with you in the room.

Step 4

Your slab, approved

You see photographs of your actual slab and approve it before it is cut, so there are no surprises when it arrives.

Step 5

Fitted and sealed

Our own fitters install it, usually within three to five working days of templating, and we take the packaging away with us.

Step 6

Aftercare inside 72 hours

If anything needs adjusting afterwards we come back, free of charge, and we have never taken more than 72 hours to get there.
