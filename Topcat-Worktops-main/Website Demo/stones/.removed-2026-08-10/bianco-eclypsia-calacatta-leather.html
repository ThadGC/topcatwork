Bianco Eclypsia Calacatta Leather

An off white ground with bold veining that carries right across the slab. Finished leathered, so there is a texture you can feel, and it hides marks a polish cannot. One long piece shows it at its best, rather than several short ones.

  • StoneQuartzite (natural stone)
  • FinishLeathered
  • CareSealed on fitting and resealed periodically. Far less porous than marble, and it does not etch the way marble does, so citrus and wine are not the worry they would be on a Calacatta.
  • In daily useA very hard stone, harder than granite on most slabs. It takes daily kitchen use well. Boards for cutting and a trivet for hot pans are still sensible.
