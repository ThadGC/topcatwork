## 1. THE DECISION THAT DROVE EVERYTHING

### ⛔ Fabrication is OUTSOURCED. The site no longer claims in-house. (REVERSAL)

**What happened.** This had been flagged as an unresolved conflict for several sessions. The
client's own sales-call notes said in bold *"Do not claim in-house fabrication anywhere on the
site"*, while Standing Rule 1 said the opposite because the client had earlier directed it. It
was blocking the SEO build, because every material, location and safety page carries a
fabrication story.

**The decision, from the client this session:** *"If it's dead, like, we don't do in house
fabrication, do not talk about in house fabrication."*

**What that means in copy, precisely:**

| TopCat genuinely does | TopCat does NOT do |
|---|---|
| Advise on and source the slab | Cut the stone |
| Laser-template the room | Polish or edge-profile it |
| Manage the whole job, one contract, one contact | Own a workshop |
| Fit and seal, with their own fitters | |
| Aftercare inside 72 hours | |
| Carry the ten year guarantee | |

**Positioning that replaced it, and why it is stronger:** *one contract, one contact, and we
answer for all of it.* The customer-psychology brief identifies "single point of accountability"
as what buyers actually value, and that claim is entirely true. TopCat never stopped being
accountable, they just do not hold the saw.

**Scope of the rewrite:** roughly 25 customer-facing claims across `index.html` and all three
sub-site builders. Verified clean: `grep -ril "our own workshop|we cut|in-house fabrication"`
returns nothing outside V2 and code comments.

⚠️ **Standing Rule 1 in `HANDOVER.md` is now WRONG and must be read as reversed.** Kept there
deliberately for now so the history is visible, but do not follow it.

⚠️ **One phrase that IS still true and was kept:** "fitted by our own team". Installation is
genuinely theirs. Do not strip it in an over-correction.

## 3. DECISIONS MADE THIS SESSION, AND THE REASONING

### 3.1 Kitchens lead, but the site is not kitchens-only
**Client, mid-session:** *"remember to not only focus on kitchens"*, then *"focusing on kitchens
are fine because it is the biggest market, but they do offer other services but kitchens should
get the most focus."*

**What changed:** I had over-indexed on kitchens. The location URL family was literally
`/kitchen-worktops/`.

- **URL renamed `/kitchen-worktops/` → `/worktops/`.** No live URLs existed yet, so no redirects
  are needed. Do not rename again once this is live without 301s.
- **A "Not only kitchens" block** was added to every material and location page, covering
  kitchens, islands, bathrooms and vanity tops, splashbacks, outdoor kitchens, commercial, and
  fireplaces/hearths/tables. It doubles as the internal link into `/services/`.
- **Kitchens still lead**, per the second instruction: they lead every title and H1, and the
  pricing tables are kitchen-based and labelled as such. `kitchen worktops {town}` is also the
  highest-volume local term in the research, so this is both honest and correct for SEO.

### 3.2 Two live rule violations found and fixed
Both were pre-existing, neither was introduced this session.

1. ⚠️ **`aggregateRating` with `"reviewCount": "16"` was in the landing page schema.** This
   directly broke the standing rule never to expose the low review count, and it was publishing
   that number to Google. It also produced nothing, because Google explicitly makes
   self-reviewed star markup ineligible. **Removed.**
2. **The landing page title was "Quartz, Marble & Granite Worktops | St Albans & London"**, which
   reflected an old, narrower positioning the client had already overridden. **Rewritten** to name
   London and the Home Counties.

### 3.3 FAQPage schema removed sitewide
Google deprecated it: FAQ rich results stopped appearing **7 May 2026**, documentation deleted
**15 June 2026**. It produced nothing and had to be kept in sync with the visible FAQ forever.
Removed from the landing page, all six service pages and the trade page.
**The visible FAQ content stays** — it still earns its place for readers and AI extraction.
⚠️ **Never add FAQPage markup to a new page.** Same for `HowTo`, `Service`,
`WebSite`+`SearchAction`, and `llms.txt`. Full reasoning in the SEO log §4.

### 3.4 Location pages built as destinations, not doorways
Google renamed "doorway pages" to "doorway abuse" and the definition turns on **funnelling**.
Enforcement is **site-level**, so a bad rollout drags the whole domain. Every location page
therefore converts standalone (prices, process, applications, phone, quote route), swaps **nine**
real local variables, and sits under a real county hub so the URL is a browseable hierarchy.
Location pages are **26% of URLs**, against a ~40% ceiling.

⚠️ **DO NOT bulk-add Phase 2 towns.** Gate at 90 days: all 8 indexed, ≥6 with impressions, ≥3
with clicks. Phase 2 and 3 lists are in the SEO log §5.

### 3.5 No LocalBusiness schema on location pages
TopCat is not located in those towns. Marking up an address it does not have risks a structured
data manual action. BreadcrumbList only.

## 4. WHAT WENT WRONG DURING THE SESSION

Recorded so it is not repeated.

1. **A missing comma blanked the FAQ.** Editing the `FAQS` array left the previous last entry
   without a trailing comma, the whole script block threw `Unexpected token '{'`, and the FAQ rail
   built zero rows. The pane's console lied about the location. **Fix adopted: a JS syntax gate**
   that extracts every inline non-JSON script, concatenates and runs `node --check`, plus
   `json.loads` on every `ld+json` block. It is in the SEO log §8 and `HANDOVER.md` §8. Run it
   after any edit to `index.html`.
2. **The comparison tables initially escaped the content column**, because the table renderer did
   not wrap output in `.block > .wrap` like every other section. Fixed in the builder.
3. **Guide `<h1>`s fell back to the body sans**, because the service shell only styles `h1` inside
   `.svc-hero-inner`. Fixed in `seo.css`.
4. **The dev server moved from port 50507 to 5501** mid-session after a restart. If `curl` returns
   `000`, check `preview_list` for the current port before assuming the site is broken.
