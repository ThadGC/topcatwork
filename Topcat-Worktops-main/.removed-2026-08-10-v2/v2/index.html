01 The idea

The surface a whole room is built around

A worktop is one of the most expensive things in the room, and it has to last twenty years. Choosing one should feel like a sure thing rather than a gamble.

Most of the trade competes on the same three lines: speed, price and warranty. We would rather compete on the part you actually live with, the quality of the work and the calm of choosing it. That means straight answers, a price that holds, and a standard of fit you can run a hand along and feel.

It also means telling you when a quartz will serve your kitchen better than the marble you came in for. It is the quickest way to earn trust, and most of the trade will not do it.

04 Origin

Every slab has a documented history

Where it was cut, the name and character of the stone, its one-off veining, and the date it was fitted in your home.

Your stone started in a quarry months before it reached you. Running through the white is a thin vein of gold, and that vein is the whole point: it is what makes the stone Calacatta Gold rather than ordinary marble, and it falls differently in every slab. We did not design it. The earth did. Our job is to choose the slab where the gold falls right, and fit it properly.

So we write it down. Every job leaves with an Origin card, which turns a worktop into something with a provenance, and gives you the one thing a catalogue photograph never can: certainty about the exact piece of stone in your room.

05 The process

From the quarry to your room

Five steps, no surprises in any of them. You will always know what happens next and what it costs.

01

Talk it through

We visit, look at the room, and listen to how you actually use it. No pressure, no jargon, and honest guidance on what suits, including when that is the cheaper material.

02

Choose your slab

You browse the collection in our online stone gallery, and on veined material we send you photographs of your actual slab to approve, so the piece you fell for is the piece that arrives.

03

A price that holds

One itemised quote covering templating, fabrication, edges, cut-outs, fitting and VAT. The number we quote is the number you pay.

04

Template & craft

Once your units are level we laser-template to a fraction of a millimetre. Your slab is precision-cut and polished in our own workshop, cut wet to full safety standards, with veining matched across every join, then finished and fitted by our own team.

05

Fitted by us

Our own fitters install it, usually within days of templating. Squared, sealed, cleaned up behind us, and ready to use.

◆

Then it is yours

You get the written guarantee, your Origin card, and a straight answer on the phone any time afterwards. That last part matters more than most of the trade admits.
