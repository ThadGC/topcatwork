### 5a. Layout architecture (READ FIRST — these are the load-bearing rules)

**Everything is normal flow now. The old "hold/arrival" choreography is GONE.** V1 used to pin a section on screen and slide/climb the next one over it (`hold-wrap`/`hold-pin`/`hold-tail`, `gal-wrap`, `proc-wrap`, `faq-wrap`, negative margins, arrival-glide IIFEs). All removed. Each boundary that used to be an overlap is now a `.section-divider`. **Do not reintroduce cross-section overlays.** Each section keeps only its OWN internal animation (see 5c).

**The clamp-floor rule — every vertical measure must keep shrinking on a short window.** A `clamp()` whose *floor* is in px stops responding the moment the screen is short enough to hit it — exactly the screen with no height to spare — so the section overruns the fold. This was the "can't see the word SCROLL" bug (`.hero-inner` had a 120px floor). Rule: pick floors small enough that the **vh term still governs at ~610px of viewport height**. Type that sets height is capped against vh as well as vw (`min(4.6vw,5.4vh)`), or a wide-but-short laptop gets max font size with the least room. **Every section fits ≤1 screen** — verified at 1200×655, 1366×610, 1512×824, 1440×900, 1920×1080. **The FAQ is the one deliberate exception** (14 questions × 3-col grid = ~2–2.5 screens by content; its `--slabH` is set by the *longest answer*, not the viewport — don't shrink it to save height or answers get inner scrollbars).

**Test sizes that matter:** always sanity-check spacing at **1200×655** (a MacBook Pro window with a bookmarks bar — the client's actual screen, reverse-engineered from title wrap) and **1366×610**, not just at 1440×900.

**Section heads are unified:** every title resolves to `.section-title` (`clamp(28px,min(4.6vw,5.4vh),54px)`) and every sub to `.section-sub` — 35px/14px with a 7–8px gap across all sections. (The FAQ title and `#process` had their own overrides; both now follow the shared rhythm.) Two subtitles that used to be `display:none` on laptops (`#stones .section-sub`, `.why-lead`) are restored and only step aside below 640px/620px.

**Backgrounds (30 July): ONE floor, nothing else full-bleed.** `body::before` (fixed grey-marble drift + two warm corner glows) is the site's only full-width texture; every section is TRANSPARENT over it (footer: translucent near-black; `#trade`'s solid ink-2 removed; `#gallery`'s own gold-marble backdrop and the `--marbleGold` token deleted). The old "blocks and cutouts" artefact was the `--marbleBG` SVG's blur-filter regions clipping (`x/y:-30% w/h:160%` percentage regions are relative to each PATH's bbox — far too tight for stroke+blur); both filters now use `filterUnits="userSpaceOnUse" x="-600" y="-600" width="2800" height="2200"`. **Do not give a section its own marble copy again** — cover-scaled duplicates at different box sizes were the patchwork the client called unprofessional. Marble on framed CARDS is fine and stays (`.hx-back`, `.cta-card`).

**Brand tokens (`:root`):** `--gold #C6A664` (champagne), `--gold-hi #E4CD92`, `--gold-lo #8C6B34`, `--bone #F4F1EA`, `--ink #0B0B0D`, `--ink-2` (card bg), `--hair rgba(198,166,100,.34)`, `--hair-soft`; `--serif` = Cinzel (display only, uppercase, short strings — never body), `--sans`/`--brand` = Montserrat; `--marbleBG` = the black-marble data-URI used on card backs / panels.

**The helix math** lives in the "SERVICES HELIX" IIFE. Sizing (29 July, client wanted the front card + spiral slightly bigger): card `w = stageW*0.56` capped 470 (was 0.52/440), `R = stageW*0.36` capped 375 (was 0.34/350) — R grows with the card so side cards keep their clearance. Per card, `d` = signed steps from front: `θ=d·60°`, `x=sin(θ)·R`, `z=(cos(θ)−1)·R`, `y=−d·STEP`, **yaw `θ·0.9`, side-to-side ONLY (no pitch — client's call, cards stay upright)**. Damped playhead (SCRUB 0.12); **`target` is unbounded** so name-clicks always take the short way round. Spiral order is the client's: `ORDER=[1,0,2,3,5,4]` over `SERVICES` = Islands, Bespoke, Splashbacks, Bathrooms, Outdoor, Commercial.

**The spiral is DRAGGABLE (30 July):** mouse/touch drag on `#helixStage` scrubs `target` directly (`hxStep` ≈ stage width × 0.33 px per card), snapping to the nearest card on release, plus a clearly-horizontal trackpad swipe steps one card per flick (2× deltaX dominance, same as the stone wheel). `hxMoved` suppresses the click that follows a drag (else releasing over a side card would goTo() it, or over the front card's link would navigate) — the flag clears on a 0-tick timeout because a drag that ends off-stage never fires a click. Cursor: grab/grabbing on the stage.

**Cards are genuinely TWO-SIDED.** At two steps out the yaw passes 90° and you see the card's **back**. ⚠️ `.helix-card` is a bare `transform-style:preserve-3d` shell — **NO overflow / filter / opacity on it**, any of those flattens the 3D and the back renders as a mirrored photo. Depth-dim and fade therefore ride CSS vars **`--hxB`** (brightness) and **`--hxO`** (opacity) which the *faces* consume, not the shell.
- `.hx-face.hx-front` = photo, name **bottom-left**, front-only "View this service" bordered button (`.hx-link`), long detail line (front only, via `--hxDetail`).
- `.hx-face.hx-back` = black marble (`--marbleBG`), gold hairline frame, stacked **TopCat / Worktops** wordmark + a small diamond.
- Both faces carry `.glow-card`; each card gets `attachGlow()` (the cursor-reactive gold border light) via a deferred `setTimeout(…,0)` — `glowItems` is a `const` declared later in the script (TDZ).

**Depth = `brightness()`, NOT opacity** (client's call — see-through cards read as ghosts). Only the wrap-around pair past |d|=2 fades out. `#services{overflow:clip}` (both axes) turns the tall solid cards into a deliberate bleed at the section edge instead of stone floating over neighbours.

### 5i. The ESTIMATOR — a cut-list engine on the client's brackets (REBUILT 2 Aug, RE-PRICED 6 Aug — read before touching)

The client asked for it to work like **cutlistoptimizer.com** — a real cut-list, not an area slider — and then gave us the real money. Stone is bought by the slab, so **slab count is the price**, and two kitchens with the same m² can cost very differently.

**The flow:** material tabs → the stone → shape chips → piece list → packer → cutting-plan ticket.

#### The price is a LOOKUP, not a build-up

```
low  = BRACKETS[worktopSlabs][island ? 'island' : 'solo'][0] + (removal ? 200 : 0) + metres*150
high = BRACKETS[worktopSlabs][island ? 'island' : 'solo'][1] + (removal ? 200 : 0) + metres*300
```

| worktop slabs | no island | with island |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | £2,000 – £2,500 | £2,300 – £2,800 |
| 2 | £3,000 – £3,600 | £3,850 – £4,300 |
| 3 | £3,850 – £4,300 | £4,350 – £5,000 |
| 4 | £4,350 – £5,000 | £5,100 – £5,550 |

- ⚠️ **The index is the slabs the WORKTOPS need, NOT counting the island.** The client's own numbers force this reading: 2-with-island is *exactly* the 3-slab solo bracket and 3-with-island is *exactly* the 4-slab solo bracket, which is "an island always adds a slab" already priced in. Under the other reading (count includes the island) three of the four island rows become unreachable and ticking `+ Island` would jump the price £1,850. **`compute()` therefore packs twice** — once with everything for the DRAWING, once with the non-island rows for the PRICE. The ticket will honestly say "3 slabs" while the bracket read was "2 + island". That is intended.
- ⚠️ **The 1-slab island row breaks that pattern** (£2,300–£2,800, not the 2-slab bracket). Built exactly as the client gave it. Flagged to them, unanswered.
- **Past four worktop slabs there is no bracket** → the price slot says **"Priced by hand"**, the plan still draws, and the meta line says "beyond the estimator". We do **not** extrapolate a fifth band.
- **Everything else rides inside the bracket.** Waterfall ends, full-height splashback and window sill are selections with **no price** — the client's instruction, and the with-island figures are stated to cover them. Only **removal (£200 flat)** and **detailed edging** move the number.
- ⚠️ **`round50` is gone.** The brackets are exact client numbers; rounding would move them.
- ⚠️ **The slab count is now the entire price, so a wasted slab is an £850 error.** `pack()` runs the same guillotine packer over **four sort orders and keeps the fewest-slab result**. Do not collapse it back to one pass.

#### Marble and Granite have no calculator

`MATS[mat].poa` is true for both. `compute()` hides `#estCalc`, shows `#estPoa`, hides the stats, draws **one whole slab of the chosen stone with a name plate**, sets the price to "Price on application", stamps the ticket "By appointment", and **hides the ticket's CTA** (the POA panel opposite already carries "Get a price for this stone" *and* the phone number, so repeating it read as a stutter). #### The rows

`label · length · width · thickness · used for · remove`, all in **mm**, built by `renderRows()`.
- **Quantity is gone** (6 Aug) — one row is one piece, which is how a cut-list actually reads, and it freed the column for thickness. **"Depth" is renamed "width"** throughout.
- **Thickness** is 20 or 30 mm per row. ⚠️ **It does not move the price** — the client's brackets do not vary by it. Captured for the quote. Worth asking them.

## 7. Verification gotchas (the in-app Browser pane is flaky with this site)

- **The pane renders emulated desktop widths SCALED when the pane itself is narrow** (it's often ~500–700px wide). Desktop screenshots then come out shrunk into a corner and hard to read. **Trust geometry measurement over screenshots:** drive `javascript_tool` to read `getBoundingClientRect()` / computed styles / `--hxO` etc. — those are exact. Use screenshots only for gross composition.
- **`window.innerWidth` reports the real pane width after `navigate`, not the emulated one.** Always `resize_window` to your target and confirm `innerWidth` before testing desktop. `navigate` resets the viewport — re-resize after it. `dispatchEvent(new Event('resize'))` after resizing forces the helix/reviews scripts to re-measure.
- **rAF playheads (helix, gallery, glow) pause when the tab is backgrounded** → screenshots can go black. `tabs_select` to re-front, or prefer synchronous JS reads. ⚠️ **CSS transitions freeze too** — `getComputedStyle` then reports the *start* value while the inline style already reads the target, which looks exactly like "my style isn't applying". Check `document.hidden`; to measure regardless, inject `*{transition:none!important}` for the duration of the test.
- **`scroll-behavior` is `smooth` on `<html>`** — setting `scrollTop` animates, so a read in the same call returns the OLD position. Set `document.documentElement.style.scrollBehavior='auto'` first, then assign, then `dispatchEvent(new Event('scroll'))` to force the scroll-driven modules to re-place synchronously.
- **A hidden pane can't paint mid-page screenshots AT ALL** (30 July): after any scroll away from the top, screenshots come back solid black and a `computer` scroll can even time out ("pane is currently hidden"). Fresh `navigate` paints once at the top. The workaround that WORKS for a visual of a mid-page section: inject a test-only style that pins the section over the viewport (`#stones{position:fixed;inset:0;z-index:9998;background:var(--ink)}` + `*{transition:none}`), force its scripts to their settled state, screenshot at scroll 0, then reload to clean up.
- **Health check after any change:** reload → `read_console_messages onlyErrors` (none) → no horizontal scroll (`scrollWidth === clientWidth`) at 1440 and 375 → every section ≤1 screen at 1200×655 and 1366×610 (process 506/531, FAQ 513/531 — both now genuinely fit; the FAQ is no longer an exception) → process: tiles land 1→5 on scroll-in, chevrons sit in the gaps, a tile click opens its detail card and Escape closes it, step 05 is not clickable → helix rotates via arrows + nav pills + side-card click AND by drag (a plain click on a side card must still rotate — the `hxMoved` suppression must not eat it), front card lands correct, back faces show the gold-framed reverse (not a mirrored photo) → process: `.flow` lands on scroll-in (line draws, nodes ignite in order), reverses when you scroll well above → stones: wheel full-bleed (belt wider than the viewport at 1440/1920), "View this stone" href tracks the centred slab (and tracks FILTERED results), Filter chips cut the wheel live (Dark+Statement in Quartz = Et Marquina alone), both rails still measure an identical 211px with all eight blocks at 190x46 and even 9px gaps, refined-to-empty shows the no-match readout with the primary disabled, Clear all restores 12/12, a `?stone=` deep link sets the estimator meta + the enquiry chip and glides the wheel to that slab, /stones/ search + filters work.
- **The pin trick for mid-page screenshots** also needs `background:var(--ink)` on the pinned section now — sections are TRANSPARENT since the background unification, so without it whatever sits under the pin bleeds through the shot.
- **No git:** confirm you only changed what you intended with a targeted `grep`/`diff`, and keep a dated `.bak` before large edits.
